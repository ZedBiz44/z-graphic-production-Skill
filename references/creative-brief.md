# Creative Brief

Capture only the facts needed to make the asset correctly.

- Asset type and intended use
- Audience and desired response
- Required dimensions or placement, when known
- Exact copy, offer, call to action, and legal text
- Approved logos, colours, fonts, photos, examples, and source links
- Elements that must remain unchanged
- Delivery channel and durable project location
- Deadline, approval status, provider limits, and cost limits

Treat supplied copy and references as authoritative. If a missing fact would materially change the asset, ask one concise question. Otherwise state a reasonable assumption and proceed.

Do not turn an ordinary request into a long intake form. A brief can be a few lines when the job is simple.
