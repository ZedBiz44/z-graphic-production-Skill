# Critique Handoff

Use this small packet when asking `z-creative-asset-critique` to review work:

- Actual asset and version
- Intended use and audience
- Exact required copy and approved references
- What must remain unchanged
- Previous findings, if this is a revision

Ask for the lightest suitable review. Ordinary work normally needs a Quick Look. Costly, high-risk, or explicitly final work may need a deeper review under the companion skill.

The critique result must include its verdict, material findings, what to preserve, and observable done-when checks. Production owns the edits and technical checks. Critique owns the quality verdict.

Passing means there is no unresolved material problem against the same brief. Reviewers do not need identical personal opinions.

Keep the exchange internal unless the requester asks to see it.
