# Production Output Checks

Check only what applies to the requested asset and its final use.

## Before Production

- Confirm the brief, exact copy, intended dimensions, and approved references.
- Identify the reusable master and final export locations.
- Confirm whether transparency, editable layers, print colour, or small-screen use matters.

## Final Export

- Open the exact file that will be delivered.
- Confirm file type, dimensions, orientation, and reasonable file size.
- Inspect crop, safe areas, clipped edges, text, logos, transparency, and obvious compression damage.
- View at the likely display size when text or small details matter.
- Confirm the filename identifies the asset and version without relying on a temporary path.

## Operation-Specific Scope

- **Create or edit:** full brief, companion critique, corrections, final-file check.
- **Resize or compress:** crop, readability, safe areas, transparency, and technical output only.
- **Resend unchanged:** correct approved file, destination, and delivery confirmation only.

Do not claim that links, QR codes, accessibility, legal rights, print settings, or platform compliance passed unless they were actually checked.
